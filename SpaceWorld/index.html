3D Space Explorer

Features: Doughnut-shaped orange-glow black hole with 13 custom planets orbiting above, plus an orbiting sun and moon!

  • W/S - Move forward/backward
  • A/D - Strafe left/right
  • Space - Move up
  • Shift - Move down
  • Left Mouse Drag - Look around
  • Right Mouse Drag - Pan camera
  • Mouse Wheel - Zoom in/out
